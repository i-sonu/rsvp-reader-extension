Privacy Policy for RSVP Reader

Effective Date: March 19, 2026

RSVP Reader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Usage:
- This extension processes data locally within the user's browser.
- No data is sent to external servers.
- No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used.

Permissions:
- The extension only uses permissions necessary for its functionality:
  * contextMenus: Required to add reading options to the right-click menu.
  * scripting: Required to inject the RSVP overlay UI into the web page.
  * activeTab: Required to temporarily access the content of the tab you invoke the extension on.
  * storage: Required to save your reading settings locally (like WPM and Themes).

Storage:
- Any stored data is kept locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API.
- This data is not shared or transmitted.

Third Parties:
- No third-party services or APIs are used.

Changes:
- This policy may be updated in the future. Changes will be reflected here.
